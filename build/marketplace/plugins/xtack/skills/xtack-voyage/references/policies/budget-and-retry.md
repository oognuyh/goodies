# Budget and retry policy

Honor explicit user and host limits on time, tokens, money, tool use, retries, and scope. Use actual host-reported usage and limits when available; do not estimate a precise remaining budget or invent billing data. If measurement or enforcement is unavailable, say what is unknown and do not claim a limit was enforced. Select proportional checks and independent work; do not spend resources on redundant reviews or rerun valid unaffected evidence without a reason. Preserve and reuse the current-target receipt while its relevant inputs and conditions remain valid. A gate result can be reused only while its target, criteria, review, CI, approval, and other relevant inputs remain valid; reevaluate affected inputs when they change.

On failure, identify whether the result indicates a product defect, a test defect, a missing dependency, or an unavailable environment. Fix an authorized defect and repeat affected checks; if attempts repeat without new evidence or progress, change the method, reduce scope to an independently useful unit, or investigate the obstacle. Respect any explicit retry limit. Do not use an arbitrary universal retry count or make a retry limit trigger an automatic question in an unattended Voyage goal.

When a real limit or unavailable capability prevents further work, preserve the Voyage goal and useful completed results, continue other authorized work where possible, and report the remaining blocker and what evidence is missing. A budget limit, interrupted run, unavailable check, or exhausted retries is not completion and does not convert a failed or unverified condition into pass. Do not promise automatic continuation unless the host actually provides it.
