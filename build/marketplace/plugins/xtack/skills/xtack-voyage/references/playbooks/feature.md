# Feature playbook

Use when a new or changed product behavior is authorized. Inspect existing contracts, users, interfaces, and constraints; establish the desired behavior, exclusions, and observable success criteria. Resolve factual gaps by investigation and reasonable documented assumptions. In unattended work, do not force an interview; a genuinely unresolved product decision that cannot be delegated blocks dependent work.

Identify relevant design alternatives and apply the ADR rule when warranted before implementation. Split a large feature into cohesive, independently verifiable units with dependencies, acceptance checks, and non-overlapping ownership. Preserve the original end-to-end outcome and integrate the units; individually passing units are not proof of their integration. Implement within scope, using focused tests or probes where useful and risk warrants them.

Run applicable basic checks, obtain the risk-required review of requirements and implementation standards against the current target, and independently execute the relevant behavior and integration checks. Connect each material acceptance criterion to actual evidence. Classify blocking findings or failures; if they reveal an incorrect contract, inconsistent requirements, inadequate design, or a superseded decision, revisit the intent and design and reassess any ADR-worthy decision before dependent changes. Correct what the user authorized without silently expanding scope, and repeat affected review and verification. Complete only when the requested feature and integration criteria are met with current evidence. Verification does not grant permission to merge, publish, or deploy.
